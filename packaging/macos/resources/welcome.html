SynthMatch

A synth that listens to a recording of a synth and builds a preset that sounds like it.

On the next pages you can choose what to install: the VST3 plugin, the Audio Unit, the standalone app, and the optional AI assistant.

The AI assistant downloads Ollama from ollama.com and the qwen3:1.7b language model (about 2.5 GB in all). They run on this Mac; nothing you type is sent anywhere else. You can skip it: matching and the node editor work without it, and you can add the model later by typing /download in the plugin.
